# Case Workbench Override

Use with `../MASTER.md`.

## Role

Investigator workspace.

## Layout

Desktop keeps three zones visible:

1. Queue and case context.
2. Evidence stack.
3. Decision and writeback.

Mobile stacks the same order and keeps evidence before final action.

## Evidence Order

1. Canonical claim facts.
2. Top risk reasons.
3. Rule hits.
4. Model and anomaly signals.
5. Provider and member context.
6. Document and medical evidence.
7. Similar confirmed cases.
8. Audit timeline.

## Acceptance

- Missing evidence can pause work without implying fraud.
- Final decisions require outcome label, notes, and evidence refs.
